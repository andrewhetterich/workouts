Block 4 · Week 4 — Rest the Leg

One genuine down-week for the hamstring — and a decision point

The Call

  • The hamstring has nagged 4 weeks and regressed to 3 — because our "protect" weeks were still a full leg-training load
  • This week the leg truly rests: no leg extensions, leg press, bridges, leg raises, squatting, or running
  • Only leg work that stays: gentle pain-free hamstring isometrics (the one evidence-based piece)

You Won't Lose Progress

  • Cardio takes 2-3 weeks of total inactivity to decline — and you're still biking
  • Strength holds even longer, and you're training upper all week
  • One rest week is recoverable in days. This is the fastest path back to running.

"Something Every Morning" — Covered

  • Shoulder's healed to 7, so upper body is back — 3 lifting days
  • Bike keeps your cardio engine (2 days) · mobility throughout
  • All of it loads the hamstring zero

⚕️ The Decision Rule

  • This rested week IS the diagnostic (since no physio right now)
  • Improves → we rebuild running/hinging slowly next week
  • Still stuck at 3 → time to get it looked at: telehealth PT, urgent care, or ask your PCP for a referral. 4+ weeks stalled isn't something to keep guessing at.

The Real Healing Lever: Sleep

  • You averaged 6.5h with two near-zero nights. Muscle heals during sleep.
  • This week, 7-8h a night does more for the hamstring than any exercise. Genuinely the priority.

If You Do OTF With Mckenzie

  • Swap it in for a bike day — but bike for cardio, upper/core for strength, sit out EVERY leg movement (squats, lunges, deadlifts, step-ups)
  • If you can't keep it leg-free, do the bike day here instead and skip OTF this week

Block 3 · Week 5 — Deload

Earned recovery after 4 weeks of building

What a Deload Is (and Isn't)

  • Same patterns, half the volume. You keep moving through every lift and run — just fewer sets, lighter loads, easy pace.
  • RPE capped at 6-7 everywhere. If a set feels hard, you've gone too heavy. Leave 3-4 reps in the tank.
  • This isn't a lazy week — it's how the adaptation from the last month actually shows up. You should finish the week feeling springy.

The Week

  • Tue — Easy Run (no threshold) + light core
  • Wed — OTF Full Body · keep it moderate
  • Thu — Pull + Posterior + Core (light, 2 sets)
  • Fri — OTF Lower Body · keep it moderate
  • Sat — Short easy long run (clean Z2 read)
  • Sun — Rest + export

OTF on a Deload

  • You can't change the class, but you can change your effort — aim RPE 6-7, don't chase the leaderboard, keep some in reserve. Enjoy it as movement, not a max.
